02 Thesis

Instrumentation as prophecy.

Every OMEN reads three things: the road, the weather and the driver. It tells you what they are about to do before they do it.

  1. 01 · ROAD

    Surface, camber and grip, sampled 200 m ahead at 2 kHz.

  2. 02 · WEATHER

    Radar, air temperature and wiper data, fused. Traction changes before the rain arrives.

  3. 03 · DRIVER

    Steering rate, brake pressure, gaze. The car learns the person, not the average.

Nothing inherited. A car that is right about the next four seconds.

03 Design language

An eclipse. Nothing else.

The oldest omen there is: a disc with its light bitten away, and one hairline through the centre, the line an instrument draws.

Every car is one side profile, drawn once and extruded. The surfaces follow the line; nothing is added to decorate it. One lamp across the nose, drawn as a hairline. One bar across the tail.
