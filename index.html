01 Heritage

One hundred and forty years of refusing to settle.

In 1886 Carl Benz filed a patent for a vehicle powered by a gas engine. It was not the fastest thing on the road — it was the only thing on the road. That instinct, to build the thing that does not yet exist rather than the thing that merely sells, has never left Stuttgart.

Meridian Autohaus exists to carry that instinct through to your driveway. We do not move metal. We place automobiles with the people who understand them.
